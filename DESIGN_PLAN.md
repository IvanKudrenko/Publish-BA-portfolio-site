# Homepage redesign plan

## Keep and reuse

- Keep the static HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture, existing routes, project links, contact links, BA logo, and project artwork.
- Keep the established reveal and mobile-navigation behavior, simplifying its presentation.
- Use the current work as the site's center of gravity and preserve the real project descriptions.

## Main changes

- Replace the gradient, floating navigation, showcase shell, nested cards, pills, and repeated feature grids with a white editorial layout.
- Lead with “I build things.” and the real 2026 working photo.
- Move projects directly below the hero. Let each original artwork act as the preview, with the whole preview clickable.
- Replace the equal numbered timeline with a short, asymmetric photo-led history: early phones, the 2016 cardboard phone, BA, and the 2025 ActiView presentation.
- Remove invented quotation styling and generic “founder portfolio” language. Keep copy short and grounded in Ivan's supplied words.
- Simplify contact into a direct closing section and plain links.

## Responsive priorities

- Compose the mobile hero as text followed by a wide image; keep the headline large without clipping.
- Use one-column project previews on phones with uncropped artwork and generous tap areas.
- Recompose story images and text into a clear vertical sequence below 760px.
- Use fluid type, gutters, and section spacing to avoid breakpoint-heavy CSS.

## Highest-impact order

1. Global type, color, navigation, and hero
2. Artwork-led project previews
3. Editorial photo story and BA statement
4. Mobile composition, focus states, reduced motion, and final visual QA
