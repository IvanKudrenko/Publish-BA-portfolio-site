My Story

Curiosity became a direction.

Early childhood

I kept imagining what devices could become.

Technology interested me before I had a plan for it. I was always looking at devices, interfaces, and how they might work differently.

Age 8

BA began as a name for the things I wanted to make.

The name stayed with me and became the shared identity behind my software, hardware, and product ideas.

Ukraine

School made room for creativity and independent thought.

At my Waldorf school in Ukraine, making and imagination were part of learning. That gave my early product ideas room to grow.

Now

The ideas are becoming working products.

At Andrews Osborne Academy and beyond, the direction became clearer. Today I am building ActiView, BlinkClip, and the wider BA product identity.
